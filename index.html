    One playlist, two people, roads that somehow always got longer when you were walking beside me.

    No big speech, no fireworks — just your fingers slipping into mine like they'd always known the way.

    Your head on my shoulder, sunlight through the leaves, and the quiet realisation that my best friend was also the love of my life.

  7. ☀

    Sunday rides to the river

    Bikes parked, shoes dusty, hands swinging between us. The best Sundays were the ones with no plan except you.

  8. ☾

    Best friend on the hard days, my love on all the days. Every single morning, I'd choose you again.
